# Vehicle Sales SQL Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes vehicle resale transaction data using SQL to evaluate revenue performance, customer behavior, and operational efficiency across quarters.

The goal was to generate executive-level KPIs to diagnose declining revenue and customer satisfaction trends.

---

## Business Questions Addressed

1. How are revenue and orders trending quarter over quarter?
2. Is customer satisfaction declining over time?
3. Which vehicle makers drive the most demand?
4. Are shipping delays impacting customer ratings?
5. What discount strategies are being used?

---

## Key Insights

- Revenue declines quarter over quarter.
- Customer ratings trend downward across quarters.
- Average shipping time increases over time.
- Certain vehicle makers dominate regional demand.
- Higher discounts correlate with certain credit card segments.
